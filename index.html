My Honor app redesign

My Role

UI/UX Designer

Team size

4-person team

Duration

5 mos 2023

Tools

Figma, Photoshop

Overview

My HONOR is a service platform for HONOR device owners, connecting device management, after-sales support, membership benefits, and product discovery.

As the app evolved to support a broader range of services and business needs, I worked on redesigning its UI and interactions to create clearer content hierarchy, more accessible service entry points, and more consistent patterns across modules.

My role: UI design, interaction design, module-level information hierarchy, visual direction, and shared component development.

I collaborated with UX designer on user flows, partnered with another UI designer to develop and synchronize shared components, and worked with stakeholders to align design goals and modular UI patterns.

the design goal

Making a growing service ecosystem more visual, scannable, and cohesive

The service ecosystem of My Honor App is comprised of:

Service flow

A portal for all-around services

Membership flow

An access to exclusive offers and benefits

Product flow

A platform for purchasing new products

Design challenge

The app brought together different user needs, from checking device information and finding after-sales services to exploring membership benefits and new products. However, many screens relied on similar text-and-icon layouts, making different types of information and actions visually indistinct.

I focused on three design challenges:

challenge #1

Making service info easier to scan

The service homepage relied on text labels and similarly styled icons, making offerings difficult to distinguish at a glance. A clearer visual hierarchy was needed to differentiate key services from secondary options.

challenge #2

Streamlining navigation and interaction

Across service support, membership benefits, and product discovery, interaction patterns needed to be more consistent and actions easier to reach. The challenge was to reduce unnecessary navigation steps and bring relevant actions closer to the information users needed.

challenge #3

Creating consistency across modules

Different flows needed to support distinct content and functions while maintaining a coherent visual language. Shared components and modular design patterns were important to keeping the experience consistent as different areas evolved.

Design decisions

Three areas of focus are as follows:

decision #1

Bring information and actions closer together

I shifted service modules beyond simple entry points by surfacing relevant information and actions directly within the page. By combining visual content, concise descriptions, and contextual CTAs, users could better understand service offerings and access relevant actions without always entering a separate flow.

decision #2

Create a clearer hierarchy and more direct paths to action

I differentiated components through visual hierarchy and established clearer patterns for prioritizing services, presenting key info, and guiding users toward next steps. This included giving key services greater prominence, separating secondary options into a dedicated catalogue, and making service discovery and actions more accessible.

decision #3

Build consistency across the app ecosystem

I collaborated with another designer to refine and synchronize shared components across multiple flows, aligning visual patterns and interaction details. We also conducted a UI/UX audit to identify inconsistencies and opportunities for improvement.

Service homepage

Bringing information and actions forward

The service homepage was redesigned to present device information and support options through more visual, informative modules. Instead of relying exclusively on text-and-icon entry points, the new layouts surfaced selected service details and relevant actions directly on the page.

Service-store discovery

Making store discovery more intuitive and actionable

Redesigned the store discovery journey to help users compare service locations, find relevant information, and take action with less friction.

Membership and benefits

Making benefits more engaging and discoverable

In order to create a more visual way to communicate information such as membership levels and service status, I explored module-level solutions using graphs, lines, and icons. These designs translated information that might otherwise be presented primarily as text into more visually structured representations.

Product & search experiences

Supporting product & service discovery

The product homepage organized device, accessory, and promotional content through modular UI layouts. The search experience used search history, recommended queries, and differentiated result components to structure the discovery experience.

Outcome and reflection

The redesign was launched as part of the app's continued development, bringing together work across its service, membership, product, and search experiences.

improvement #1

Improved service discoverability

Informal user feedback suggested that key services were easier to find, supported by clearer visual hierarchy and more prominent entry points.

improvement #2

More accessible service journeys

Prioritized frequently used services and refined store discovery and detail pages, helping users navigate toward relevant services and appointment options more directly.

improvement #3

More consistent UI patterns

Established and refined reusable UI components and modular design patterns to create greater visual consistency across service, membership, product, and search experiences.

The project reinforced the importance of designing beyond individual screens: a cohesive product experience depends on how modules connect, how users move between tasks, and how shared patterns support consistency as the platform grows.

Sola Salons

My Role

UI/UX Designer

Team size

3-person team

Duration

2 mos 2024

Tools

Figma, Photoshop

Overview

Sola Salons is a salon booking platform that helps customers discover nearby locations, compare salon professionals, and book appointments. The project focused on simplifying the booking journey by creating a more intuitive search experience and guiding users through a clear sequence of decisions.

the design goal

A more seamless booking experience

Create a cohesive, efficient booking experience that helps users find the right salon, check professional availability, and book an appointment with confidence.

Design challenge

The existing booking experience presented friction across the search and selection journey. Fragmented entry points, inconsistent information hierarchy, and unclear navigation made it difficult for users to compare salons and find suitable appointment times. The challenge was to create a more cohesive, predictable flow while making key information easier to find at each step.

challenge #1

Fragmented search entry points

Salon discovery was spread across multiple entry points, including the homepage and individual salon pages. Each led to a different search path, making the experience inconsistent and creating unnecessary navigation.

challenge #2

Unclear information hierarchy

Search results lacked a consistent UI pattern and clear information hierarchy. Important details were not organized around users’ key decisions, making it harder to scan results, compare locations, and identify relevant information.

challenge #3

An unpredictable booking journey

The relationship between location search, filters, stylist selection, and appointment availability was unclear. Users had limited guidance on what to do next or what to expect at each step, while finding an available appointment could require unnecessary navigation.

Design decisions

I organized the experience around a progressive, location-first journey, allowing users to narrow their options in a logical order—from finding a suitable salon to choosing a professional and booking an available time.

Start with Location

Help users discover nearby salons that offer their selected service, establishing location as the starting point for the booking journey.

Make salon comparison easier

Present locations with a consistent info hierarchy, supported by maps, filters, and key salon details, so users can evaluate their options before moving forward.

Guiding from selection to booking

Make stylist availability and appointment times accessible after a location is selected, reducing unnecessary navigation and clarifying the next step.

Design and prototype

Design Strategy

Progressive, location-first booking

Consolidated fragmented search entry points into a more consistent journey. Users begin by finding salons that match their location and service needs, then move through location selection, professional selection, and appointment booking.

Search Result Screen

Clear information hierarchy

Established a more consistent layout and UI pattern across search results. Prioritized essential information, such as location, service relevance, and key salon details, to help users scan results and compare options more easily. Maps and filters support exploration without competing with the primary selection task.

Salon Services Screen

Simplified decision-making

Separated the booking process into focused steps, each supporting one primary decision: where to go, which professional to choose, and when to book. This reduces the cognitive load of evaluating multiple options at once and makes the journey more predictable.

Dev Considerations

Efficient availability loading

Worked within the stakeholder’s technical constraint of a large stylist-availability database. Rather than loading all availability data during initial search, the flow retrieves relevant availability after a user selects a salon. This reduces unnecessary data requests and supports a more efficient, step-by-step experience.

Outcome and reflection

improvement #1

A more cohesive search experience

Consolidating the search journey creates a consistent path from discovery to booking, replacing disconnected entry points with a more unified experience.

improvement #2

Clearer decisions at each step

A stronger information hierarchy and progressive flow help users understand their options, compare locations, and move toward an appointment with less uncertainty.

improvement #3

UX aligned with technical constraints

Loading availability only when needed connects the user journey with backend data constraints, demonstrating how interaction design can balance usability, performance, and system requirements.

The project reinforced the value of designing complex workflows around users’ decisions, not just the information a system needs to display.

AI-powered banking experience

My Role

UI/UX Designer

Team size

Solo designer

Duration

1 week 2026

Tools

Figma, Photoshop

Overview

As part of a one-week design assignment for a mainstream bank, I explored how AI could make cross-border banking more intuitive and accessible. I researched emerging AI tools and reviewed international transfer flows across banking apps to identify opportunities to simplify complex financial tasks through conversational interaction.

the design goal

Making cross-border banking more conversational, transparent, and effortless through AI

Design an AI-assisted transfer experience that helps users express their financial intent in natural language, understand the costs involved, and complete transactions with confidence while maintaining control over important decisions.

User group

Global Chinese clients living abroad who regularly manage finances across countries and currencies.

User need

A convenient and reliable way to manage cross-border transfers without navigating complex banking workflows or seeking in-person assistance.

Core challenge

International transfers involve recipient, accounts, FX, fees. Users need a simpler way to complete these tasks without sacrificing transparency or security.

Design challenge

Cross-border transfers require users to navigate financial complexity while making decisions that directly affect their money. The challenge was to reduce the effort required to complete a transfer while ensuring that AI assistance remained transparent, predictable, and trustworthy.

challenge #1

Complex fees & exchange rates

International transfers can involve multiple fees, exchange-rate fluctuations, and different receiving amounts. Users need to understand the total cost and expected outcome before committing to a transaction.

challenge #2

Time-consuming banking workflows

Traditional banking flows often require users to navigate multiple screens, manually enter information, and repeatedly review details. For users managing finances across time zones, completing routine transfers should require less effort and fewer steps.

challenge #3

Security & trust in financial decisions

Financial transactions require accuracy, transparency, and explicit authorization. While AI can help interpret intent and prepare a transaction, users must be able to verify critical details, correct mistakes, and retain control over the final decision.

Design decisions

Rather than requiring users to navigate banking menus and complete forms from the start, I explored a conversational approach in which users express their financial goals and the AI guides them through the necessary steps.

The broader opportunity was to help users understand and manage financial tasks through simple, contextual conversations—while using structured interfaces whenever precision and control matter most.

Understand user intent

Allow users to express a goal naturally, such as “Send CAD 5,000 to my mother in Shanghai.” The AI interprets the request and identifies the information required to initiate the transfer.

Anticipate user needs

Use available context, such as saved recipients and frequently used accounts, to reduce repetitive input. When information is ambiguous, ask for clarification rather than making assumptions.

Simplify user input

Combine conversational interaction with quick actions and structured components, allowing users to describe their needs naturally while retaining familiar ways to select, enter, and edit financial details.

Provide contextual guide

Explain FX, fees, recipient details, and transfer status at relevant moments in the journey. Help users understand the implications of their choices before they authorize a transaction.

Design and prototype

step #1

AI landing & chat interface

The landing page introduces the AI assistant through quick actions and recent activities, giving users clear entry points to common banking tasks. Within the chat interface, users can express their intent naturally, while processing indicators communicate when the AI is working on a request.

This approach lowers the barrier to starting a task without requiring users to understand the banking information architecture in advance.

step #2

Account, fees & exchange rate

A combination of conversational messages and structured UI components supports different levels of interaction. The AI can interpret requests and guide users through the process, while structured cards present account options, recipient details, transfer amounts, fees, and exchange rates in a format that is easier to scan and compare.

This hybrid approach combines the flexibility of conversation with the precision needed for financial decisions, allowing users to review and edit transaction details before proceeding.

step #3

Transaction flow & confirmation

Before completing a transfer, users can review the transaction summary and make final adjustments to important details. Passcode authorization provides an explicit confirmation step before the transaction is submitted.

By separating AI-assisted preparation from user authorization, the flow reduces interaction effort while keeping the user responsible for the final financial decision.

Outcome and reflection

reflection #1

Balancing flexibility with control

Conversational AI can make banking tasks more accessible, but natural language alone is not sufficient for complex transactions. The most effective interaction combines flexible conversation with structured information and clear decision points, allowing users to explore, correct, and confirm their choices.

reflection #2

Designing for confidence & security

Trust depends on more than a helpful AI response. Users need to understand what the system has interpreted, which details it has retrieved, what costs are involved, and what will happen next. For high-stakes tasks, the interface should make critical information visible and require explicit authorization before execution.

reflection #3

Trust takes more than seamless UX

Financial tasks involve established habits, perceived risk, and a strong need for reliability. A smoother interface is only the starting point; users also need consistent behavior, clear feedback, and predictable recovery when information is missing or incorrect.

As a one-week concept assignment, this project focused on exploring the interaction model and translating it into a prototype. The next step would be to validate whether users understand the AI’s suggestions, feel confident reviewing transaction details, and can recover easily when the system misunderstands their intent.

Overview

The HONOR 90 product page translates the device’s refined industrial design and functional advancement into an immersive digital experience. My focus was on elevating product storytelling through elegant visuals, motion, and interactive exploration.

Using Figma for both static design and interactive prototyping, I explored transitions, animations, and interaction patterns to bring the product’s visual qualities to life.

the design goal

Craft an immersive web experience

Create a visually engaging journey that invites users to explore the HONOR 90 beyond a conventional product page.

Motion-first experience

Use purposeful animation and transitions to create continuity between sections and make product features feel dynamic.

Visual storytelling

Translate the device’s design details, materials, and features into a cohesive visual narrative.

Responsive interaction

Make product exploration intuitive and engaging through interactive elements that encourage users to discover more.

Creative concept

The concept centered on turning product discovery into a visual journey. Rather than presenting specifications as isolated information, the page uses cinematic layouts, carefully paced transitions, and interactive moments to connect product details with the overall brand experience.

Creative concept

Gallery experience

Designed an immersive gallery journey through carefully composed layouts, smooth transitions, and motion. Each section builds on the previous one, creating a cohesive experience that guides users through the product’s design and features.

Creative concept

Enhanced interaction

Introduced interactive moments that encourage active exploration, including zooming into product details and triggering interactions with the 3D phone model. These interactions give users a closer look at the device while making product discovery more engaging.

Creative concept

Premium visual language

Used refined typography, generous spacing, high-quality product imagery, and controlled motion to reinforce the HONOR 90’s premium positioning. Visual consistency across sections helps create a polished, cohesive experience.

Design and prototype

static mockup

Static UI design

Developed high-fidelity screens in Figma to establish the visual direction, layout hierarchy, typography, and composition. The static designs provided a clear foundation for evaluating the page’s overall look and feel.

interactive prototype

Interactive prototyping

Built interactive prototypes in Figma to communicate the creative concept and demonstrate transitions, animations, and key interactions. The prototypes supported stakeholder alignment, design iteration, and creative sign-off, while providing a reference for development handoff and implementation discussions.

Outcome and reflection

Outcome #1

Stronger visual storytelling

Connected product details, imagery, and motion into a more cohesive digital narrative.

Outcome #2

Elevated brand perception

Used refined visual design and purposeful interactions to support the HONOR 90’s premium product positioning.

Outcome #3

A more engaging product journey

Explored ways to move beyond passive browsing, allowing users to interact with the product and discover its details.
